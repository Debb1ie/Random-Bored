Learn Something
Feed your brain without the pressure of a syllabus. 10–30 min chunks that actually stick.
Free Solo Brain food
  • Watch a Kurzgesagt or Veritasium video on a topic you know nothing about
  • Open Wikipedia and click "Random article" — follow 3 links deep
  • Learn one Duolingo lesson in a language you've always wanted to try
  • Pick a word in another language and memorize it with a silly story
  • Read one long-form article on The Conversation or Aeon
  • Try a free Khan Academy exercise on something you skipped in class
  • Search "how does [random object near you] work" — go down the rabbit hole
🎨
Create Something
You don't have to be good at art to make things. Make something weird. Make something small.
Creative Any skill level
  • Write a 6-word story about your day (seriously, just 6 words)
  • Doodle on paper for 10 minutes with zero plan
  • Take 5 interesting photos around you right now — look for light and shadows
  • Open Google Slides and make a presentation about your pet or a show you like
  • Write the first paragraph of a story set in your school — but it's a thriller
  • Make a playlist that tells a story — title each section like a chapter
  • Design your dream bedroom layout on paper (include a secret room)
🏃
Move Your Body
You've been sitting for hours. Your back knows it. Do literally anything physical for 10 minutes.
Active Feels good
  • Do a 10-minute stretching routine — search "office stretch routine" on YouTube
  • Walk around the block once while listening to a podcast
  • Do 3 sets of 10 jumping jacks, 10 squats, 10 push-ups — rest, repeat
  • Put on a random song and actually dance to the whole thing
  • Do the 7-minute scientific workout (search it — it's a real thing)
  • Stand outside for 5 minutes, breathe, look at something far away
  • Learn one TikTok dance routine (no judgment)
🎮
Play & Explore
Guilt-free games and rabbit holes. These are legitimately fun and some are secretly educational.
Fun Solo or Group
  • Play GeoGuessr — try to guess where in the world you are from Street View
  • Try the NYT Wordle, Connections, or Spelling Bee
  • Play Typeracer or Monkeytype to see how fast you type
  • Chess.com puzzle of the day — takes 5 minutes, exercises your brain
  • Try "The Impossible Quiz" online — it's stupid and amazing
  • Watch a SpeedRun video of your favorite game or movie
  • Open Google Earth and "visit" a place you want to go someday
💬
Connect with People
Text someone you haven't talked to in a while. Boredom is better shared.
Social Low effort
  • Text a friend a random funny memory you both shared
  • Start a group chat debate: "Is a hot dog a sandwich?" — go
  • Send a voice note instead of a text to someone you miss
  • Watch the same YouTube video simultaneously with a friend and react in real-time
  • Play a browser game with a friend like skribbl.io or garticphone.com
  • Write an appreciation text to someone who'd be surprised to get one
  • Start a collaborative Spotify playlist with a friend and add 5 songs each
🌱
Do Future-You a Favor
Small things you've been putting off that take under 15 minutes. Future you says thanks.
Productive 15 min
  • Clean up your desktop and downloads folder — delete what you don't need
  • Write down 3 things you want to do this week, then mark one as "today"
  • Unsubscribe from 5 newsletters or promo emails you never open
  • Drink a full glass of water. Then another. You're probably dehydrated.
  • Organize your notes app or study folder — label things properly
  • Look up one scholarship or opportunity related to what you want to study
  • Write 3 things you're genuinely proud of this month, no matter how small
